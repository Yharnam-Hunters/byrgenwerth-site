Character

Every character instance in the game is updated once a frame. This page covers one of those updates: throw events, the regeneration of a points stat, and the hand-off of frame time to the character's modules.

At a glance

Scope
character_frame_update_01cbdb20, one of the per-frame updates of a character instance.
Verified
character_frame_update_01cbdb20 is verified: the throw event, the points regeneration with its fractional carry, and the frame-time hand-off.
Open
Whether the points stat is stamina, whether the update belongs only to the player, and the rest of the instance layout.

Summary

Character instances (ChrIns in FromSoftware's engine) are updated once a frame through virtual calls on each instance. character_frame_update_01cbdb20 (0x01cbdb20) is one of these updates.

What the update does

  1. Fires a pending throw animation event (W_ThrowAtk or W_ThrowDef) and clears the request.
  2. Regenerates a points stat that floors at -50 and caps at a maximum (see below). It reads like stamina.
  3. Hands the frame time to the character's modules, copies a few parameters, and builds (then drops) a wide string of the character's name with the thread's allocator. Nothing reads the string; it looks like the remains of debug code. In the recordings from Iosefka's Clinic the name string is never built.

How the points stat regenerates

The regeneration rate is a product of four terms:

Text Only
rate = parameter byte × 0.01 × per-character multiplier × virtual factor

The multiplier lives at +0x16c on the instance, and the last rate is kept at +0x39c. Each frame the update:

  1. multiplies the rate by the frame time (read from +0xe0);
  2. adds the whole part of the result to the points (data module +0x134), capped at the maximum (+0x138);
  3. keeps the fraction at +0x168 and adds it in on the next frame.

Carrying the fraction means a rate too small to add a whole point in one frame still adds up over several frames instead of being rounded away.

Two switches stop the stat from dropping: bit 7 of the data module's +0x200, and the debug flag 0x0593e88a next to the WorldChrManDbg singleton.

Points, stamina and the player

The source calls this value points. Its relationship to stamina, and whether this update belongs only to the player, are not established. The current evidence-backed account is in Stats & Progression.

Scope still to map: the character instance layout, the modules (+0x3b0), the parameters, the other per-frame updates.

Data module (modules +0x20): points +0x134, maximum +0x138, a no-drop flag at bit 7 of +0x200.

0x0593e88a: a debug flag (next to the WorldChrManDbg singleton 0x0593e880) that stops the stat from dropping.

Patch points

The frame-rate patches (see Frame timing) replace the frame time read from +0xe0 with a constant:

Patch Frame time used
30 FPS++ 1/27 s
60 FPS++ 1/54 s
60FPS (no deltatime) 1/50 s
90 FPS++ 1/80 s
Uncap FPS++ the real frame time

BB_TARGET_FPS does the same for 30 and 60. A fixed time slightly longer than one frame (1/27 instead of 1/30) speeds regeneration up a little; why the patch authors chose it is an open question.

Open questions

  • Is the points stat stamina? A floor below zero would fit a stat that can be overspent.
  • Why the patches choose 1/27 and 1/54 rather than 1/30 and 1/60.
