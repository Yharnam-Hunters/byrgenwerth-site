AI

The game runs a shared Havok AI world, owned by the SprjHkAiManager singleton. This page covers the once-a-frame update that steps that world and draws its debug display.

At a glance

Scope
The per-frame AI manager update: the world step and the AI debug drawing.
Verified
ai_hk_frame_update_0222bc10 steps the AI world by a fixed 1/30 s and gates the debug drawing behind flags.
Open
The world step itself (0x021cdad0), the groups and objects, and how individual enemies decide what to do.

Summary

The game steps a shared Havok AI world through SprjHkAiManager (singleton 0x059401a0). The verified wrapper does not establish which enemy or NPC decisions that world supplies.

What one frame does

Once a frame, ai_hk_frame_update_0222bc10 (0x0222bc10):

  1. Steps the Havok AI world with a fixed time step of 1/30 s.
  2. Checks its second flag. It is always set in the recordings. When it is set, the update:
    1. copies the frame object's matrices to the manager's view object;
    2. resets the render manager's debug state to its defaults and runs the AI debug display and drawing, made of the pieces below.
Piece What it uses
Debug position the camera's position, or the player character's when the character manager exists
Debug marks (not yet detailed)
Draw descriptor every AI object in the owner's groups, or the whole manager
Axis frames ids looked up in the manager's id map
Boxes the box mode at +0x2c68

The time step is a constant, not the measured frame time. Every frame moves the AI world on by 1/30 s however long the frame really took. That is why the frame-rate patches have to change it (see Patch points).

In the recordings from Iosefka's Clinic the update resets its three groups, copies the matrices and draws each AI object; the debug display, axis frames, boxes and the manager-wide draw stay off.

Scope still to map: the AI world step itself (0x021cdad0), the groups and objects, the behaviour of individual enemies.

What this wrapper does not tell us

This verified wrapper establishes how the AI manager is stepped and how its debug drawing is gated. It does not reveal an individual enemy's decisions, attack timing, or boss phases. The current source and evidence contain no basis for those profiles yet; gameplay observations alone will be labeled as observations until their implementation is traced.

Structs and globals

Owner of the update. The AI objects sit two levels down: the owner holds groups, each group holds elements, and each element holds a list of objects.

Offset Field
+0x2bc8 group count
+0x2bd0 groups, 0x20 bytes each: element count +0x8, elements +0x10
(element) 0xb0 bytes each: object count +0x58, object list +0x60
+0x2c18, +0x2c20 debug display
+0x2c68 box mode
+0x2c90..+0x2c98 flags
+0x2cb8 axis-frame pairs (id, index), +0x2ec0 of them

SprjHkAiManager. AI world +0x10 (its id map at +0x10), view object +0x20.

Patch points

The AI world's time step is 1/30 s. The frame-rate patches are described in Frame timing.

Patch AI world step
original, 30 FPS++, Uncap FPS++ 1/30 s
60 FPS++, 90 FPS++, 60FPS (no deltatime) 1/60 s
