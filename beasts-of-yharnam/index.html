Beasts of Yharnam

This section organizes future research on enemies, hunters, and non-player characters. It is a guide to what the project can currently support, not a claim that individual behavior has already been decoded.

What the source tells us so far

Paleblood has verified the outer AI manager update: ai_hk_frame_update_0222bc10 advances the shared Havok AI world and controls a debug-drawing path. The AI world's internal update, object identities, and individual decisions are not yet understood. See the AI system page for the verified details and limits.

Research paths

  • Enemy Directory sets the evidence requirements for individual profiles.
  • Enemy AI & Behavior covers the difference between visible behavior and an established implementation.
  • NPCs & Hunters explains how those profiles will be kept distinct from enemy and boss profiles.

The Character, Event, and Physics pages contain verified shared-system findings, but none currently identifies a specific enemy or NPC.
