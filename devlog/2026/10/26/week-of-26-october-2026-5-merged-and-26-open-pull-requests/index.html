Tooling and automation

open#100 tools: bound overflow arguments in scripted callbacks, by @evill33t. Open pull request: proposed, not merged.

  • Findings: Observe explicitly selected SysV integer/pointer overflow arguments in import and internal callbacks. Validate selectors before execution, preserve the entry stack pointer, and turn callback access errors into failed results.

open#101 tools: validate raw SSE fixture input bytes, by @evill33t. Open pull request: proposed, not merged.

  • Findings: Treat XMM fixture inputs as bounded raw register bytes. Reject malformed or oversized values before execution and preserve upper-lane payloads.

merged#102 tools: skip byte scans of unchanged harness snapshots, by @evill33t. Merged 2026-10-28.

  • Findings: Skip Python byte-by-byte scanning when the before/after writable snapshots are identical. Changed snapshots continue through the existing difference extraction.

merged#98 tools: prepare extracted backups without copying assets, by @evill33t. Merged 2026-10-28.

  • Findings: Accept an already extracted backup, convert only its executable modules, and avoid duplicating game assets. Reject overlapping or unsafe output paths and propagate conversion/target failures.

open#99 tools: add resumable private REA function analysis, by @evill33t. Open pull request: proposed, not merged.

  • Findings: Keep REA images and decompiler output outside the public checkout. Resume only hash- and profile-matching analyses, recover complete retained evidence after transport limits, and preserve cleanup failures.
